Readers-Writers Problem

Rules:

  • Multiple Readers can read simultaneously
  • A Writer has exclusive access (no readers and no other writers)
  • Readers and Writers cannot be active simultaneously

Challenges:

  • Readers Priority: Writers may wait indefinitely (starvation)
  • Writers Priority: Readers may wait indefinitely
  • Fair: FIFO admission with finite service; no indefinite queue bypass

Admission policy

Logical lock acquisition is atomic in this model. Reader priority admits waiting readers whenever no writer owns the lock; writer priority stops new readers while a writer waits; fair admission serves the FIFO request queue, batching adjacent readers. Changing policy affects subsequent grants and does not revoke active holders. The reader-count pseudocode illustrates the reader-priority protocol; the other policies add the admission queue described here.
