2 Processes Software Solution

Classical algorithm assumptions

Peterson assumes two participants, indivisible shared reads/writes and sequential consistency, finite critical sections and eventual scheduling of enabled steps. With both flags true and turn=0, P0 may enter and P1 waits. The badges observe this finite trace; they are not a proof for every execution. Ordinary unsynchronised C variables do not implement this memory model; use a proven mutex or a correctly ordered atomic implementation.

How Does It Work?

variables:

  • flag[i] = true means process i wants to enter the critical section
  • turn = the favoured process when both flags are true; the other process yields

Logic:

  • 1. Set your flag to announce intent
  • 2. Favour the other participant (turn = other)
  • 3. Wait while the other participant requests entry and is favoured
  • 4. Clear your flag when leaving
