Evidence and dimensional limits

This page evaluates fixed source constants and performs no CPU/GPU/NPU benchmark. Their empirical provenance is unresolved; the author must choose whether to retain a fully specified illustrative dataset or replace it with reproducible measurements. N/A means unsupported or not dimensionally comparable, not zero cost. Nominal device specifications and workload power inputs are different quantities. Intel Core Ultra 7 165H: NPU peak INT8 11 TOPS, overall peak INT8 34 TOPS (official specification). The original 15 W NPU value is not a verified component measurement. Intel lists the i9-13900K at 125 W base power and 253 W maximum turbo power, while NVIDIA lists RTX 4090 total graphics power as 450 W; these are different specification boundaries, not the measured workload watts.
