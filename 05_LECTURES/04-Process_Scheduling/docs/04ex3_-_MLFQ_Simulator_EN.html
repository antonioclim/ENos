Declared MLFQ policy

Strict priority between queues. Upper queues use round robin with their configured quantum. The lowest queue is FCFS. Higher-priority arrival preempts a lower-level job and preserves its used quantum; I/O-bound work yields after half its configured quantum, with zero simulated I/O latency, and retains its cumulative level budget. Exhausting that budget demotes upper-level work even if it would also yield. The lowest level has no quantum expiry. A priority boost moves unfinished active work to Q0 and resets all quantum budgets. These are explicit rules of this teaching model.

MLFQ Rules

  1. A new process enters the highest-priority queue (Q0)
  2. A process that exhausts its cumulative level allotment is demoted, except at the final queue
  3. Yielding for I/O preserves its level and already-used allotment
  4. The highest-priority non-empty ready queue runs first (Q0 > Q1 > ...)
  5. Boost: Periodically move unfinished work to Q0 and reset allotments; eventual service requires finite load and fair order
