Scheduling assumptions

One CPU, known service times and no context-switch cost are assumed. Non-preemptive SJF minimises average waiting for a known simultaneous batch under those assumptions; it is not universally optimal with arbitrary arrivals. Example: P1 arrives at 0 with burst 10 and P2 at 1 with burst 1; work-conserving SJF starts P1 and P2 waits 9 units, whereas permitted idling until 1 changes the optimum. SRTF is preemptive and relies on known remaining service.
